8:14 PM

TLDR:
- You will make a dating profile, choosing one of three traits for four separate categories. After you @ production to approve your profile, it will be locked in and you may not change any traits. - You will attempt to match yourself and your traits to a Moviesona. The closer your traits are to theirs, the more points you get. - You will attempt to match your date to a location. The closer THEIR traits are to the location’s, the more points you get. - You will attempt to clear that location’s mini game. The less time you take to complete said mini game in comparison to your date’s time, the more points you get. - The player with the most love points, and by definition the date that was the most successful, wins the first individual immunity of Snackvivor: Concession Cove!
