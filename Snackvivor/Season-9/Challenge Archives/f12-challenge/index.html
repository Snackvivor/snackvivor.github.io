Spooky? Perhaps! But anyways, for those not in the know, Fibbage is a game where you have prompts and throw in lies to trip up your fellow truth finders
8:21 PM
This isn’t exactly Fibbage to a tee, there’s been some minor alterations, so listen up! First Phase! In your submissions channel, you will receive a prompt. No making up a fun fact here, all of these questions will be something you can answer on a personal level. You should answer this question truthfully, as you will get points for every person that correctly identifies the truth (more details later). And nooooooo, you can’t say hey this is my answer. Second Phase! Next, you will receive 5 prompts that aren’t your own. This is where you are trying to answer the questions in a way that will stump the other castaways and make them think the person in the prompt said your answer. You want a really good lie here. Third Phase! Finally, the 5 other prompts will be handed back to you in a pretty picture with your lies and 5 other answers! Your goal is to find the truth for each prompt and avoid those dastardly little fibs. And that’s all there really is to it, nothing too complex. Scoring Also very simple! For each truth you find, you get 3 points. For each person that finds the truth on your prompt, you get 2 points. And for each person you stump with a lie, you get 1 point per person fooled. Tiebreaker will follow the order of truths found>people that found your answer>people you stumped, and placement (since you all decided to divide yourselves evenly how nice of you thank you thank you) will be determined by total points on each tribe. One more side note, it’s fine to beef up your prompts a little. I don’t want the entirety of the Bee Movie script as an answer, but I did make the images large enough to be able to zoom in if you need to read a larger answer. Because of the simplicity of this challenge, ideally the first phase is done later tonight and the other two phases can be completed later in the day tomorrow. Other than that, I hope you enjoy it and let me know if you have any questions!
8:22 PM
Also! If there are two or more people that submit the exact same answer, you both will be asked to change your answer once. Nothing like time of submissions matter, think of it like... phase 2.5.
