Grace 5/17/2025 4:20 AM
Okay so it’s not my ghost story, but my partner’s grandmother used to work at a care home and she has a lot of very interesting superstitions. Sometimes they are just relevant on holidays, like on new years we have to get fresh water from a nearby river and sprinkle it around the house to cleanse everything. But her biggest one, and most relevant one is that when someone is about to die, you have to open a window in the room, so their soul has a way to escape! I thought that was a bit odd when she told me that, as it’s not something I had ever heard before, so I asked her what would happen if that wasn’t done? And she told me about this one time where someone had died right before her shift, so she wasn’t there to open the window and the person was new, so they didn’t know to. And when she walked into the room, there was a tall man, dressed in all black, with a tall top hat on that was leaning over the deceased person. He looked up at my partners grandmother when she walked in the room, but the second she went to tell him to move away from the body or ask him who he was, he disappeared. Obviously, she was a bit shaken up, but she was pretty sure that it must’ve just been her imagination playing tricks on her and she didn’t actually see that man. But later when she was walking around and visiting with some other residents, quite a few of them had seen that man as well! They always reported seeing him walk by their rooms when they were having an emergency or pretty sure they were about to die. Nobody had really any guesses for who he was or why he kept hanging around, but all the residents who had seen him agreed that they did not like the feeling of being around him. So to this day we have no clue who he was or what he is doing there, but residents still report seeing him and my partners grandmother is even more cautious about making sure the window is open now :)
