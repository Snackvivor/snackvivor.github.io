# F10 - The Great Escape
8:28 PM
Alright so let me explain the challenge for you all (edited)
8:29 PM
The duos will work on a maze together with partial information: One person will have the horizontal walls, while one person will have vertical walls.
8:30 PM
When a duo is ready to begin, one player will put !f10-horizontal in their submissions and the other will put !f10-vertical.
8:30 PM
In a specified channel, the two players will take turns moving in a direction in the maze. If they accidentally hit a wall, they will instead not move. At any point, they may ping production to ask for their position in the maze, but a one-minute timer will be set that must be finished before you may ask again.
8:30 PM
The duos may decide who goes first, you start in the top left corner inside the maze. Since you must alternate, the first player may say "Move X" and that will move the duo in the maze as they share the same space. The second player may then say "Move Y" and continue on like that. If there is ever a point where someone gives directions more than once and do not alternate, any subsequent directions will be ignored until the other player takes their turn.
8:30 PM
Directions that can be moved are Left, Right, Up, Down
8:31 PM
Once the challenge begins, players may not speak anywhere outside of submissions other than giving directions and asking for your position.
8:31 PM
You have a maximum of thirty minutes to complete this challenge - if you do not complete it, your score will be determined by the number of steps before the exit.
8:31 PM
Ping production when you are ready to begin as you will need a member to run it with you.
