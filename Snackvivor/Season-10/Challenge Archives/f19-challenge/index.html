# F19 - Ruined Raddle
8:14 PM
8:14 PM
RADDLE is a word transformation ladder game where your goal is to figure out which clues go with the previous or next word on the ladder!
8:14 PM
8:14 PM
Each rung on the ladder changes the previous word or phrase into a new word or phrase, by applying one of the clues. For example, you may see clues asking for things like these:
8:14 PM
8:14 PM
The transformation may be a simple letter removal THORN → HORN It may be a more complex change, like anagramming THORN → NORTH It might be a change based on the word's meaning THORN → ROSE
8:14 PM
The clues are your guidance to figuring out which transformation is the correct one to go from one rung to the next!
8:14 PM
Also, unlike a normal word ladder you can see how many words there are meant to be and how long each word will be in each spot!
8:14 PM
8:15 PM
### How To Play
8:15 PM
8:15 PM
Look at the starting word, ending word, and the set of clues provided
8:15 PM
8:15 PM
Only one clue will help you transform the current word to the next word. Your job is to figure out which one!
8:15 PM
8:15 PM
You must build the full word ladder from top to bottom by using all of the provided clues, you can only use each clue once!
8:15 PM
8:15 PM

### What's Different (Kinda)
8:16 PM
8:16 PM
Unlike the original RADDLE, there are NO HINTS but you CAN work from anywhere on the ladder as you see fit!
8:16 PM
For submissions, you must submit the entire ladder at the end to be scored from top to bottom

8:16 PM
### Example Submission
8:17 PM
8:17 PM
Example Submission from RADDLE to NUTS: RADDLE WADDLE DEE DEEZ NUTS
8:17 PM
Scoring is pretty simple, for each word in the right position, you get a point! If you tie for maximum points, the fastest time to finish (determined by when you submitted the full list) will win! When you are done with your submission please say DONE
8:17 PM

IMPORTANT - FOR REAL READ THIS Also, when reading the clues follow this logic - Anything in the clues that are in "____" refers to the previous word, while anything that only has a ____ (No quotes) is the word you are trying to solve for...

8:18 PM
When you are ready, you can run !f19-raddle-off in your submissions to get started! You will get two images, one that is all of the clues (out of order) and the other is the list from top to bottom with information on the number of letters for each word in-between
