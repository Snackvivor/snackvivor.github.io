My review of the longest joke ever (I will be putting this in submissions too I just want the spectators to have a chance to read it) The longest joke in the world is a fantastically done Norm MacDonald-style shaggy dog joke in which a long, weaving story is told to set up one very simple punchline. I’ve actually heard a much shorter version of this joke in which most of the pretense about Nate and the special lever are set up in about two sentences, and it’s just a nameless man driving nearby that has to swerve and hit Nate instead of the lever. This version was obviously much better because of the story weaved. During the beginning of the joke, I found myself not really relating to the protagonist all that much, as it seemed that he was smart enough to bottle and drink his wiper fluid and wayfind by the stars but stupid enough to have gotten himself stranded in the desert to begin with. I found some of the writing to be trite (image 1), but there were some parts of the story that I found to be even more comedic than the punchline (images 2 and 3), which is the hallmark of a truly successful shaggy dog joke. Overall, I found the desert-wandering stage of the joke to be very poetic and harrowing, the dialogue between Jack and Nate after meeting to be realistic and compelling, and the later stage of Jack returning to civilization and later taking Sammy the snake on a globetrotting journey to be a fun way to inject some more humor into the end of the joke. My least favorite part was the typo (image 4). Overall I give this joke five stars and am grateful for having read it ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ (edited)
