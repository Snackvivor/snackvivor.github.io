Fish The Second 2/20/2026 9:26 AM
So, it's what I would call DnD-lite in that when you enter combat with someone (either you get attacked or you attack) you enter into a separate channel that is just for combat. In that channel you have a turn order and when its your turn, you can run !attack @player weapon and assuming you have the weapon and picked the right player and its your turn you roll a d20 behind the scenes that includes your buffs / nerfs. If you beat the other player's armor class (Default of 11) then you hit them. When you hit them, your weapon has a damage roll it can do, which is anywhere from 1 to the weapons max damage. So if a bow has a max of 3, you would roll either a 1, 2 or 3 and then hit them for that much damage as your turn. Then it goes to the next person in the turn order. There's also something to be said about attacking someone while they are in game sleeping (or IRL sleeping). If you attack an awake character, there is a roll to see who goes first which is called initiative (that's a DnD thing). If they are sleeping, you guarantee go first. Anyone who joins combat after the first 2 just jumps to the end of the turn order. If you are fighting someone who is not Discord available in the moment they get a 6 hour grace period to check. Then you can skip their turn. After that, the grace period shrinks to like 3 hours, then to 1 hour, then you can just freely keep skipping them. The follow up skips are things you need prod to okay, but we get notified when battles happen and will keep track of that so dont worry. It's a little annoying to have to wait 6 hours to maybe miss, we get that, but it's kinda the only good way to give grace to people not being chronically online while also still letting people who jump you have some kind of advantage
