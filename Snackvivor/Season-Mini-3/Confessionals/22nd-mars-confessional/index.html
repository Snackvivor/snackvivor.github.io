Mars jumped from his podium as the games began, second delayed but swift on his feet all the same. He didnt have time to look around or explore - he and his district partners had a plan and he would stick to it. Quickly, Mars raced towards the Cornicopia and picked up the first thing he found - an axe! He gripped the hard wood in his mouth and ran south, just as he, Connor and Becca had predicted. Screams and clashing sounded behind him but he never turned. He didnt have the time. His heart was pounding, paws slamming against the hard ground as he raced away. Finally, certain that he had escaped the cornucopia, Mars dropped the axe from his mouth and spun around, search first for Becca. No sign. Concerned, he looked around the trees. Nothing. "BECCA?" He called, concerned that there was no sight of his close friend rushing through the trees. His heart raced faster as the adrenaline was replaced with fear. "Connor?" He whispered. Mars turned to see Connor emerge from the Cornicopua, knife in hand. One partner at least was alive. Cannon fires Mars jumped at the sound, his ears pinned to the back of his heard at the sudden loudness of it all. Everything was too loud here. Just as others rounded the corner, Mars sat down on his axe, hiding it from view. Sunder and Jah entered the area, their eyes on alert as they looked around. Mars raised his hackles, scared of a fight but prepared if he needed. Surely Becca would come around the corner. She just wanted to search more. Surely she was alive.

# Day 2 #
Mars 2/23/2026 12:59 PM
The stars shine over the arena as Mars blearily opens his eyes and stretches from where he slept on Connor's chest. If he were to predict the time, it felt like roughly 3 am for him, although others might call it 5am depending from where they were. They weren't like Mars though - he lived in Cat Time Zone, which meant that 3 am was perfect time for Arena Zoomies. Mars jumped down from Connor and landed with a soft thunk on the ground. Around him slept other tributes, people who were just as scared as he was that they would be jumped in the night and made a sleep pile for safety. Not allies. Nobody was really allies - nobody but Connor. Mars softly moved through the pile of sleeping bodies, his eyes scanning each of their faces - faces that he would see in the sky over the course of the next few days. With each falling of his paw, he prayed he would see Becca's face resting amongst the others. As if she had found them was just sleeping. Wherever she was in the arena... Mars didn't think she was sleeping. Mars left the people behind, moving through the Sandwiches that littered this part of the arena and moved down towards the arena. If ever there was a time to search without fear of being jumped, now seemed like as good a time as ever. Mars searched through every BLT, Hoagie, Grilled Cheese and Cheesteak that littered this part of the arena, stopping every so often to take a taste of them while he searched. His mother always said that cheese would upset his stomach, but she was dead now, so what did she really know? In one of the BLT's, Mars reached in underneath the bacon and nearly jumped with joy when he felt batteries beneath the bacon. He quickly picked them back up and sprinted back to Connor, hiding the batteries and settling back onto the warmth of Connor's head, resting once more

Mars 2/24/2026 10:53 AM

They had timers for movement and co-ordinated that everyone was ready. Mars watched them closely, never once opening his mouth to speak in fear they may notice him and attack. Camryn and Crow slept nearby - his new allies. Together the 4 of them were working to keep each other safe. But they were nothing like this group. "Tonight, District 12 dies!" One of them cried, and as quickly as they arrived the entire group was gone, disappeared lower into the arena. Mars watched as they cross the cornicopia and moved further south.
