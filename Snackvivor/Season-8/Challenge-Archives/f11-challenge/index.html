If I put Deadpan, I’d get no points. - This applies for all four categories. The maximum number of points you can receive is EIGHT. - There will be hints in the Cuisinesona’s description, use them wisely!
8:02 PM
## PHASE TWO - DATE NIGHT:
8:02 PM
- Next, you gotta take your lovely Cuisinesona on a date! - There will be eight locations for you to choose from, each with their own set of preferred traits. You must guess, based on your Cuisinesona’s traits and the hints provided on the location card, which place your Cuisinesona would like to go the best! - Each Mascot also has an OPTIMAL LOCATION. If you take your Mascot to their perfect date place, you will get an extra two love points on top of the eight you would receive for a perfect match! 10 in total! - Very similar to our first phase! But this time, the full location card is not revealed to you - So you won’t know how many points you received this round. - But that’s not all, because each date has its own activity that you must complete there.
8:03 PM
## PHASE THREE - STRESS TO IMPRESS:
8:03 PM
- The activity that you’ll be completing on the date will also be hinted at in the location description, so you’ll have to prepare yourself for what’s ahead! - Your Mascot will have a set time that they will have completed the location’s activity in - You want to go as fast as possible. If your time is under the mascot’s by 30 seconds or more, you will receive eight points. If your time is within 30 seconds over or under the mascot’s, you will receive four points. If your time is over the mascot’s by 30 seconds or more, you will receive two points. - For example, let’s say that the location I take Mana to has a slide puzzle mini-game! - After inserting a command, I will complete the activity and submit my time! - For example, I completed my Slide Puzzle in 6:37. It is then revealed that Mana’s time was 6:15. That’s WITHIN 30 seconds of Mana’s time, so I get four points! - You will not be told your partner’s time. You will complete your mini game and the date will be over. Exact times, comparisons, and final point totals will be revealed during results.
8:04 PM
The player who received the most love points on their date wins! You will also be asked to guess the total amount of love points every player will receive across the board. This will be our tiebreaker.
8:04 PM
TLDR:
- You will make a dating profile, choosing one of three traits for four separate categories. After you @ production to approve your profile, it will be locked in and you may not change any traits. - You will attempt to match yourself and your traits to a Cuisinesona. The closer your traits are to theirs, the more points you get. - You will attempt to match your date to a location. The closer THEIR traits are to the location’s, the more points you get. - You will attempt to clear that location’s mini game. The less time you take to complete said mini game in comparison to your date’s time, the more points you get. - The player with the most love points, and by definition the date that was the most successful, wins the first individual immunity of Snackvivor: Cuisine Clash!
