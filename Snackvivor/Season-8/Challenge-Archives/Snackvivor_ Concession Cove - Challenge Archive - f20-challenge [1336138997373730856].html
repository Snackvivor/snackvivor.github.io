THATS MY WIKI RACE
8:23 PM
In this challenge, you all will be pairing up with someone. Once you have the pairs you will participate in a good ole fashion wiki race….but with a twist. For those not in the know, a Wiki Race essentially entails getting from one article on Wikipedia to another in as few clicks as possible. On two of the teams, both players will know the starting clue but one of the players will not know the ending word of the chain. Both players will take turns clicking on links on the Wikipedia page to other Wikipedia pages to try and get to that final word. When either person gets to the ending word, the person who knows the end word says “DONE” or something similar, and moves on to the next race. To help out a bit each chain will also have a hint to help guide in the direction of the end goal. You will not be able to talk to each other about the challenge to help out, you can only send links back and forth. There will be four chains in total. Once you have decided pairs and who knows the end page and who doesn’t, we will provide you with the information required. The player who knows both words will be the one to first click a link after the starting word. An example of how this will work: Let’s say the chain is peacock to tiger. Player A knows the starting word is peacock and the ending word is tiger. Player B knows the first word is peacock and that's it. Both players also know the hint, which in this case was orange. Player A will click the first link on the Peacock page and post that in their partner’s 1-1. Then player B finds a link on that page, sends that to player A, and so on and so forth until the target page is found by either player.
8:23 PM
The last group will be doing a more scheduling-friendly version of the above, a Wiki-Walk if you will. In this group, you will be doing each race independently, knowing both the start and end pages. Scoring for each race will be determined by whoever clicked the least amount of links between the two of you. As an example, person A clicked 6 times, person B clicked 4, your score for the first race would be 4, you get it.
8:23 PM
The tribe with the least total links clicked will be the winner with time being a tiebreaker (but only for the 2 duo runs). You don’t have to do these runs consecutively as tiebreaker will be measured by when you get to the end word in each instance. Please ping us when you have your groups and sitouts sorted, and if you have any questions please ask and happy hunting!!
