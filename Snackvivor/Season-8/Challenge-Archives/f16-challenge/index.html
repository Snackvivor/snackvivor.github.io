# F16: Q-Less Clash!!
8:06 PM
In this game, you'll be given a handful of letters, and your goal is to create words that connect, like a scrabble board.
8:07 PM

Basic Rules

  • You must use ALL of the letters given to you to create your board (this will be possible, we've checked the letter pools)
  • Words must be 3 letters or longer
  • Letters are not worth more or less points, neither are longer words. Your only goal is to create a board as quickly as possible
8:07 PM
There will be 5 different groups of letters.
  • Group A will have 8 letters
  • Group B will have 10 letters
  • Group C will have 12 letters
  • Group D will have 14 letters
  • Group E will have 16 letters
8:07 PM
One person from each tribe will have to choose a level, and will compete against the other people in their group. Your tribe will earn 2 points for 1st, 1 point for 2nd, and 0 points for 3rd. The two tribes with the most amount of points will win immunity, and the other tribe will be headed to tribal!
8:07 PM
In case of ties, we will use time as a tiebreaker.
8:08 PM
Please discuss among your tribes who will be doing which group, and ping me with a list as soon as possible, after which I'll send you the command for your level!
