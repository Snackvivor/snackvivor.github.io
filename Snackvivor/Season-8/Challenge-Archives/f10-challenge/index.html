Before you start to solve, you will need to make sure you do each of the following: 1) Click/Tap “Practice” (DO NOT DO THE DAILY). 2) Click/Tap the Settings (gear) icon in the top right. 3) Solve Assist should be OFF. SCORING
8:33 PM
Scoring will be decided based on the following criteria, in order: 1) Number of words successfully solved (this is counted for both “failed” and “solved” puzzles). 2) In the case of a tie-breaker, it will go to the person who solved the words in the least amount of guesses. 3) If an additional tie-breaker is needed from there, it will go to the person who solved it the fastest (thus the need for the speedrun timer setting).
