Code Names First up we have a modified game of Codenames. One of you will be given a board of a bunch of words with some words colored. These are the words you are trying to get your partner to guess. Since this is single player, we need to modify it a bit! The clue setter must give a clue that is one word like normal. But the guesser must always guess a minimum of 2 words. The clue setter will also premake all of their clues then the guesser will get each clue individually. So they will get one guess then if they can keep going they will. The clues will be 1 word each and you cannot use words on the board. The guesser will keep going until they pick a word that is not one of the chosen words where they will be forced to stop with the points they have collected so far. However if the guesser guesses the assassin word they will stop and get 0 points.

Wavelengths Finally, we have Wavelengths. One of you will receive a pair of two words that are on opposite sides of a spectrum alongside a number from 1 to 100. Your goal will be to give a two word clue for each spectrum that your partner will then guess to try and get as close as possible to the given number. For example you may get the spectrum COLD - WARM with the number 80 and you may use the clue HOT DAY and the closer your partner gets to 80 the better the points. The scoring is as follows. WITHIN 5 - 3 Points 6 - 10 off - 2 Points 11 - 15 off - 1 Point Anything more than that is NOTHIN. Hosts can reject some clues at will depending on how they are worded.
