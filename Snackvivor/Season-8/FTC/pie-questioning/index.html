4th Place: First Time - Hozier Those few who remember my last two rankings will be VERY surprised to be Hozier this high. Hozier has appeared on every playlist I've ranked, and in the other two they ranked very very low. But alas, Hozier has finally broken me. And it broke me very slowly. I heard it the first time and thought it was another boring Hozier song. A little catchier than most but mostly just uninteresting. But then I noticed the accent. I didn't realize that Hozier was Irish until then. And thought caused me to listen closer to his vocal performance, and the lyrics of the song itself. And I realized then that this song is actually awesome. Never before have the lyrics of a song changed my opinion of a song so much. It's a beautiful song reminiscing about lost love. Mourning the loss, but also celebrating the joys that were had. It's bittersweet, but the message in it that I took is more sweet than bitter. It's the first time a Hozier song really clicked with me, and it clicked hard. I know this song is about romantic love, which is something I've never really had, but it can easily be made about any sort love that's been lost for any sort of reason, and that's something anyone can relate to no matter who you are. Highlights: I want to give special shout-out to the verses, specifically the first two. I really think beautifully poetic in a lot of ways. From the first one, a line I really love is "The soul, if that's what you call it, uneasy ally of the body. It felt nameless as a river undiscovered underground." Which hit me really hard for reasons I think should be obvious. And basically the entirety of the second verse where he waxes poetic about flowers in a vase, that is essentially an allegory for the entirety of the message of the song. And he ends it with "but anyway..." as if it was just some spur-of-the-moment philosophical musing borne from whatever emotions had been wrapped up in the story he's retelling. I think that's just awesome.

4th Place: First Time - Hozier Those few who remember my last two rankings will be VERY surprised to be Hozier this high. Hozier has appeared on every playlist I've ranked, and in the other two they ranked very very low. But alas, Hozier has finally broken me. And it broke me very slowly. I heard it the first time and thought it was another boring Hozier song. A little catchier than most but mostly just uninteresting. But then I noticed the accent. I didn't realize that Hozier was Irish until then. And thought caused me to listen closer to his vocal performance, and the lyrics of the song itself. And I realized then that this song is actually awesome. Never before have the lyrics of a song changed my opinion of a song so much. It's a beautiful song reminiscing about lost love. Mourning the loss, but also celebrating the joys that were had. It's bittersweet, but the message in it that I took is more sweet than bitter. It's the first time a Hozier song really clicked with me, and it clicked hard. I know this song is about romantic love, which is something I've never really had, but it can easily be made about any sort love that's been lost for any sort of reason, and that's something anyone can relate to no matter who you are. Highlights: I want to give special shout-out to the verses, specifically the first two. I really think beautifully poetic in a lot of ways. From the first one, a line I really love is "The soul, if that's what you call it, uneasy ally of the body. It felt nameless as a river undiscovered underground." Which hit me really hard for reasons I think should be obvious. And basically the entirety of the second verse where he waxes poetic about flowers in a vase, that is essentially an allegory for the entirety of the message of the song. And he ends it with "but anyway..." as if it was just some spur-of-the-moment philosophical musing borne from whatever emotions had been wrapped up in the story he's retelling. I think that's just awesome.
